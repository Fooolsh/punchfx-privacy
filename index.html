PunchFX 私隱政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 31 日

PunchFX 係一個 Wear OS 手錶 app：開始 Session 之後偵測揮拳，偵測到就播放音效。我哋嘅原則好簡單：PunchFX 唔會收集、儲存喺我哋嘅伺服器、或者分享你嘅任何個人資料。PunchFX 冇帳戶、冇廣告、冇分析或追蹤工具，亦冇任何伺服器。

動作感應器數據

PunchFX 只喺你撳「開始」之後讀取手錶嘅加速度計，用嚟即時判斷係咪出咗拳。數據只喺手錶上即時處理，唔會儲存、唔會傳出手錶。撳「停止」、自動停止或者離開 Session 之後就唔會再讀取。

音效檔

你可以用「上傳音效」功能，喺同一個 Wi-Fi 網絡入面嘅瀏覽器將音檔傳去手錶。音檔由你部裝置經本地網絡直接傳去手錶，只會儲存喺手錶入面，唔會經過或者傳去我哋或任何第三方。上傳頁只喺你打開嗰陣運作，並要輸入手錶顯示嘅一次性代碼。你可以隨時喺上傳頁刪除音效，解除安裝 app 亦會刪除全部音效。

設定

靈敏度、音量等設定只儲存喺手錶本機。

權限用途

  • 通知、前景服務（健康類型）、保持喚醒：Session 期間令 app 喺熄屏後繼續偵測，並喺錶面顯示運行中圖示。PunchFX 唔會讀取或寫入 Health Connect、Samsung Health 或任何健康紀錄。
  • 網絡、Wi-Fi：只用嚟喺本地網絡接收你上傳嘅音效。
  • 震動：偵測到出拳時嘅觸覺回饋。

兒童

PunchFX 唔係為 13 歲以下兒童設計，亦唔會收集任何人嘅個人資料。

PunchFX Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026

PunchFX is a Wear OS watch app that detects punches during a session you start and plays a sound effect. PunchFX does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics or tracking, and no backend servers.

Motion sensor data

PunchFX reads the watch's accelerometer only after you tap Start, to detect punches in real time. The data is processed on the watch only and is never stored or transmitted. Reading stops when you tap Stop, when the session auto-stops, or when the session ends.

Sound files

With "Upload sounds", you can send audio files to the watch from a browser on the same Wi-Fi network. Files go directly from your device to the watch over your local network and are stored only on the watch; they are never sent to us or any third party. The upload page runs only while it is open on the watch and requires a one-time code shown on the watch. You can delete uploaded sounds at any time, and uninstalling the app removes them.

Settings

Settings such as sensitivity and volume are stored only on the watch.

How permissions are used

  • Notifications, foreground service (health type), wake lock: keep punch detection running with the screen off during a session and show an ongoing-activity icon. PunchFX does not read or write Health Connect, Samsung Health, or any health records.
  • Network and Wi-Fi: only to receive sounds you upload over your local network.
  • Vibration: haptic feedback when a punch is detected.

Children

PunchFX is not directed to children under 13 and does not collect personal data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
